# AgriSahay – Multilingual Agriculture Banking Glossary

This standardized glossary establishes consistent, culturally appropriate terminology across all 10 supported Indian languages in AgriSahay.

| English Master Term | Hindi (हिन्दी) | Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) | Tamil (தமிழ்) | Telugu (తెలుగు) | Banking Context & Usage Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Loan** | ऋण | ಸಾಲ | கடன் | రుణం | Agricultural credit facility (e.g. KCC, Term Loan) |
| **Repayment** | पुनर्भुगतान | ಮರುಪಾವತಿ | மறுசெலுத்துகை | తిరిగి చెల్లింపు | Harvest-aligned bullet or seasonal debt service |
| **Interest** | ब्याज | ಬಡ್ಡಿ | வட்டி | వడ్డీ | Annual lending rate or subvention rebate |
| **Insurance** | फसल बीमा | ಬೆಳೆ ವಿಮೆ | பயிர் காப்பீடு | పంట బీమా | PMFBY crop calamity insurance protection |
| **Crop** | फसल | ಬೆಳೆ | பயிர் | పంట | Cultivated produce (Sugarcane, Paddy, Ragi, etc.) |
| **Field Visit** | खेत निरीक्षण | ಕ್ಷೇತ್ರ ಭೇಟಿ | கள ஆய்வு | క్షేత్ర తనిఖీ | On-site farm inspection by Relationship Officer |
| **Approval** | स्वीकृति / संस्तुति | ಅನುಮೋದನೆ | ஒப்புதல் | ఆమోదం | Branch Manager credit sanction authorization |
| **Rejection** | अस्वीकृति | ತಿರಸ್ಕಾರ | நிராகரிப்பு | తిరస్కరణ | Formal denial of loan application with reason |
| **Pending** | लंबित | ಬಾಕಿ | நிலுவையில் | పెండింగ్ | Awaiting action, inspection, or dual sanction |
| **Overdue** | अतिदेय (विलंबित) | ಮೀರಿದ ಬಾಕಿ | காலாவதியானது | గడువు ముగిసింది | Default / dues past harvest clearing date |
| **Risk** | जोखिम | ಅಪಾಯ | அபாயம் | రిస్క్ | Agronomic and credit exposure classification |
| **Audit** | ऑडिट / लेखापरीक्षण | ಆಡಿಟ್ | தணிக்கை | ఆడిట్ | Immutable compliance and regulatory ledger |
| **Consent** | सहमति | ಸಮ್ಮತಿ | ஒப்புதல் | సమ్మతి | DPDP Act compliant borrower data authorization |
| **Offline Sync** | ऑफ़लाइन सिंक | ಆಫ್‌ಲೈನ್ ಸಿಂಕ್ | ஆஃப்லைன் ஒத்திசைவு | ఆఫ్‌లైన్ సమకాలీకరణ | Local queue reconciliation with core server |
